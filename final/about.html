About Me

Producer & Editor

Kate Fugitt is an award-winning producer and editor currently studying Film & Television at NYU. She is interested in producing, post-production, live television, and finding creative ways to tell stories that feel personal, organized, and visually engaging.

During her time at NYU, Kate has worked across student films, internships, film festivals, and live production spaces. She has experience managing production logistics, editing short-form content, supporting live shows, organizing events, and collaborating with creative teams under tight deadlines.

Her goal is to build a career in film and television where she can combine creative storytelling with the behind-the-scenes organization that helps productions actually happen.
